My Journey

I am Nahma Fathima, currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science with an Artificial Intelligence specialisation at NIAT. My enthusiasm for technology stems from a genuine desire to understand how modern intelligent systems function, how software bridges abstract logic with practical utility, and how physical computing intersects with robotics.

Alongside programming, I have a deep creative passion for drawing and painting. Practicing physical art instills an eye for detail, balance, and aesthetic harmony that directly informs how I approach software design and interface creation.

During an NIAT bootcamp, I developed my first technology project, Digital Second Brain (DSD), and I actively participate in community tech activities like TinkerHub, where I explore robotics and collaborative hands-on projects with peers.
